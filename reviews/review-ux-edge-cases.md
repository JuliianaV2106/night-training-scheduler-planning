| E-8 | At 1440×, a preset 60 s before a deadline is 42 ms of real time, so the operator never sees the instant the preset exists for. No rule for jumping backwards. | High | **Accept (partially Reject)** | **Accept:** a preset jump **auto-pauses** on arrival, so the operator resumes at the rate of their choice (1× recommended). The rationale is corrected to "60 s before the Ramp". **Reject (backwards jump):** time never moves backwards, by design. The simulator is a forward-only discrete-event engine (PRD FR-11, NFR-7 determinism), so a "rewind" is a **new run with the same seed** (`POST /simulations`), not a jump. This is stated explicitly in the spec rather than added as a feature. |

## Summary

- **Accepted fixes are applied** to the owning files listed in each row; the PRD change is limited to FR-2 rule (k) (E-3a).
- **One partial rejection (E-8):** rewinding simulated time contradicts the PRD's forward-only, deterministic engine (FR-11, NFR-7). Replay is a new seeded run.
- The two **Critical** findings on time boundaries (E-2, E-4) and the one on capacity (E-1) directly strengthen the mandatory edge case (checkpoint failure during morning eviction) and Scenario 3. They are the reason this review was run after the wireframes were drawn rather than before.
